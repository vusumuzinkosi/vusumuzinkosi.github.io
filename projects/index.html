Forecasting

Eskom Demand Forecasting: How Much Electricity Will South Africa Need Next Week?

An hour-by-hour, week-ahead forecast of national electricity demand from 4.5 years of Eskom data, using calendar, public-holiday and lagged-demand features, with no weather data.

  • 3.1%error a week ahead, 15% better than “same as last week”
  • 2.4%error a day ahead, against Eskom's own 2.3%
  • 52weeks of walk-forward testing
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Gradient boosting
  • Walk-forward validation

Fraud detection

Fraud Detection: A Daily Review Queue for Mobile Money

Turns 2.6 million mobile money transactions into a daily review queue sized to a six-analyst fraud team, using only information known at transaction time and a time-based split, after removing a leaking balance column.

  • 98%of fraud cases caught (89% of the money)
  • 87%of flagged transactions are genuine fraud
  • 300reviews a day, matching team capacity
  • Python
  • scikit-learn

Customer retention

Churn Prediction: Revenue at Risk

Tells a retention team whom to call first. A random forest with a decision threshold set from the business cost of each error, then customers ranked by expected loss rather than churn probability alone. A live Streamlit planner rebuilds the call list for any team capacity and costs.

  • 95%of churners caught (ROC AUC 0.98)
  • 82%of value at risk protected at 10% contact capacity
  • 58%if ranked by churn probability instead
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Random forest
  • Cost-based threshold
  • Streamlit

Customer analytics

Customer Segmentation: RFM and K-means for Targeted Campaigns

Replaces one-size-fits-all email with four customer segments, each with its own campaign. RFM tiers built by hand as a baseline, then K-means on log-scaled RFM, validated on the following six months of spending. The cleaning and RFM steps are rebuilt in SQL (DuckDB) and match the pandas results exactly.

  • 19%of customers bring in 72% of revenue
  • 4segments, each with a proposed campaign
  • 19×more spent by the top segment than lapsed buyers
  • Python
  • pandas
  • scikit-learn
  • K-means
  • SQL (DuckDB)
