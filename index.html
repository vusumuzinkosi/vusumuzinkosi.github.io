Projects

Five end-to-end projects. Each one starts from a business question, beats a simple baseline, and ends with a decision a team could act on.

Forecasting

Eskom Demand Forecasting: How Much Electricity Will South Africa Need Next Week?

An hour-by-hour, week-ahead forecast of national electricity demand from 4.5 years of Eskom data, using calendar, public-holiday and lagged-demand features, with no weather data.

  • 3.1%error a week ahead, 15% better than “same as last week”
  • 2.4%error a day ahead, against Eskom's own 2.3%
  • 52weeks of walk-forward testing
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Gradient boosting
  • Walk-forward validation

Fraud detection

Fraud Detection: A Daily Review Queue for Mobile Money

Turns 2.6 million mobile money transactions into a daily review queue sized to a six-analyst fraud team, using only information known at transaction time and a time-based split, after removing a leaking balance column.

  • 98%of fraud cases caught (89% of the money)
  • 87%of flagged transactions are genuine fraud
  • 300reviews a day, matching team capacity
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Logistic regression
  • Imbalanced data
View code and write-up

Customer retention

Churn Prediction: Revenue at Risk

Tells a retention team whom to call first. A random forest with a decision threshold set from the business cost of each error, then customers ranked by expected loss rather than churn probability alone.

  • 95%of churners caught (ROC AUC 0.98)
  • 82%of value at risk protected at 10% contact capacity
  • 58%if ranked by churn probability instead
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Random forest
  • Cost-based threshold

Completed

A/B Test: Should the Online Store Roll Out the New Landing Page?

A pre-registered test on 290,584 visitors with a clear answer: don't roll out. The new page converted 0.16 points lower (p = 0.19, 95% CI −0.39 to +0.08), and with 98% power the whole interval sits below the +0.5-point lift worth acting on.

  • Python
  • statsmodels
  • SciPy
  • Hypothesis testing

Customer Segmentation: RFM and K-means for Targeted Campaigns

Four segments from 5,852 customers and two years of transactions, each with its own campaign. Loyal big spenders are 19% of customers and 72% of revenue, and segments built on older data predicted the next six months: the top group spent 19× as much as lapsed buyers.
