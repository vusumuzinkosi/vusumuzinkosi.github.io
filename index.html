03. Projects

Five end-to-end projects. Each one starts from a business question and ends with a decision someone could act on.

Completed

Eskom Demand Forecasting: How Much Electricity Will South Africa Need Next Week?

Hour-by-hour, week-ahead forecast of national demand from 4.5 years of Eskom data. 3.1% MAPE a week ahead, 15% better than “same as last week”; 2.4% a day ahead against Eskom's own published 2.3%, without weather data.

  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Gradient boosting
  • Walk-forward validation

Completed

Fraud Detection: A Daily Review Queue for Mobile Money

Turns 2.6 million mobile money transactions into a daily review queue sized to a six-analyst team. Reviewing the top 300 a day catches 98% of fraud cases (89% of the money), with 87% of flags genuine, after catching a leaking balance column.

  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Logistic regression
  • Imbalanced data

Completed

Churn Prediction: Revenue at Risk

Tells a retention team whom to call first. The model catches 95% of churners on unseen customers (ROC AUC 0.98), and ranking by expected loss protects 82% of the value at risk with capacity for 10% of customers, against 58% when ranking by churn probability.

  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Random forest
  • Cost-based threshold

Completed

A/B Test: Should the Online Store Roll Out the New Landing Page?

A pre-registered test on 290,584 visitors with a clear answer: don't roll out. The new page converted 0.16 points lower (p = 0.19, 95% CI −0.39 to +0.08), and with 98% power the whole interval sits below the +0.5-point lift worth acting on.

  • Python
  • statsmodels
  • SciPy
  • Hypothesis testing

Completed

Customer Segmentation: RFM and K-means for Targeted Campaigns

Four segments from 5,852 customers and two years of transactions, each with its own campaign. Loyal big spenders are 19% of customers and 72% of revenue, and segments built on older data predicted the next six months: the top group spent 19× as much as lapsed buyers.

  • Python
  • pandas
  • scikit-learn
  • K-means
