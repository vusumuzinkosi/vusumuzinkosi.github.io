About

Mathematics teacher turned data scientist.

I spent more than nine years helping students make sense of numbers. Now I help teams make decisions with them.

I'm a data scientist based in Newcastle, KwaZulu-Natal, completing a Postgraduate Diploma in Data Science at STADIO (NQF 8). Before moving into data science I spent more than nine years teaching mathematics and IT, most recently as a Senior Lecturer at Majuba TVET College, where I already used learner performance data to spot at-risk students early.

That background shapes how I work. I begin with the decision a team needs to make and what a wrong answer would cost, compare every model with a simple baseline, validate on data the model has never seen, and size the output to what people can actually act on. Then I explain the result so a manager, not only another analyst, can use it. I like work where the analysis ends up inside something people use every day.

I'm looking for data scientist and data analyst roles in South Africa or remote. Get in touch or download my resume.
